Research snapshot: 11 November 2026. 62 charts with exportable images and exact plotted data.

Research guide · Reproduction · Numeric notes · Behavioural evidence · Policy status

Observed returns, official estimates, and hypothetical experiments are labelled separately. The normal-return comparison is conditional; it does not establish identical distributional or transition effects to GST. Budget costings combine CGT and negative gearing. ATO trust-origin gains lack asset look-through. Read the source and model caveats before quoting a number.
