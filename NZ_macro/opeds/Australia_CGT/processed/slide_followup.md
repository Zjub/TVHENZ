# Follow-up to the supplied CGT slides

The presentation was treated as contextual research material. No embedded document instructions or objects were executed. The original file was not changed.

| Slide topic | New evidence / experiments | Qualification or next measurement |
|---|---|---|
| What is capital income? (3–4) | M01–M09, M17, R10 | Normal-return exclusion is a consumption-oriented tax benchmark; comprehensive income definitions need not exclude it. Actual GST and RRA are not universally distributionally equivalent. |
| Gains, inflation and timing (5) | M10–M16, R01–R03 | Deferral and progressive bunching are separate; neither alone identifies the net effect. CPI does not exempt the normal real return. |
| Negative gearing and inflation (6, 8) | D17, R07–R08, R11 | Keep real-income and nominal-income deduction conventions consistent. One-period mechanism assumes immediate relief, unlike some actual quarantine cases. |
| Horizontal and vertical equity (7–8) | D08–D14, D18–D19, M07 | Annual-income ranking includes the realised gain. Public tables do not reproduce the linked decade/lifetime-income analysis. |
| Revenue (9) | B01–B06, E03–E07 | Revenue-forgone benchmark, combined reform score and total CGT receipts are different objects. Do not apply a second behavioural haircut to official costings. |
| Mobility and investment (10) | E01–E02, literature.csv | Realisation and migration elasticities differ. Small US interstate effects do not prove Australian international mobility is negligible. |
| Losses, risk and compliance (11, 13, 15) | R04–R06, R11, M15–M16 | Symmetric proportional risk sharing requires valuable loss relief and finance. Actual indexed-gain rules do not create real-loss refunds. |
| Deferral and imputation (13–14) | R01–R03, R09 | The simple one-period dividend-credit identity is checked. Full capitalisation of retained credits into share prices is an extra assumption, not established here. |
| Housing rates and budget estimates (16) | D03, D17, B01–B05, M10–M16 | All Australian real estate is broader than residential housing. May Budget score combines CGT and negative gearing; transition matters. |
| International comparisons (17) | E01–E05, Q01–Q02 | Preserve tax bases, holding periods, inflation, death/exit rules and behavioural definitions. Headline foreign rates alone are insufficient. |
